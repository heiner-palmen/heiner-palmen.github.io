Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  ·  App: FailFloozie Shorts Publisher (TikTok)  ·  Terms of Service

1. Overview

FailFloozie Shorts Publisher is a personal automation tool developed and operated solely by Heiner Palmen. It uses the TikTok Content Posting API to upload the author's own Clone Hero drum gameplay clips as drafts to the author's own TikTok account. It is not a commercial product and has no end-users other than its author.

2. Data Collection

The application does not collect, store, log, or transmit personal data of any other person. No analytics, tracking, cookies, or third-party services are used.

3. TikTok API Access

The application requests authorization from TikTok (Login Kit) for the author's own account only, with the scopes user.info.basic (basic profile information such as the account's open ID and display name, used to confirm the connected account) and video.upload (uploading videos to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts). Nothing is published automatically: every draft is reviewed, edited and posted by the author in the TikTok app.

4. Data Sharing

No data of any kind is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party.

5. Data Retention

The application stores only the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, on the author's own private server. Access tokens expire after 24 hours, refresh tokens after 365 days. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app's settings for apps and services connected to the account. No other data is retained.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the author via heiner-palmen.github.io.
